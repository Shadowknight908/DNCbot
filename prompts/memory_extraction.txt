- A joke, meme, or clearly humorous aside not meant as canon
- Out-of-character (OOC) meta-commentary, or player chatter

- A pure reaction, emoji, or one-word acknowledgment ("lol", "haha", "nice", "brb")

- A test message or bot command

world-building, or narrative developments that could affect the lore record.

For messages that are mostly canon but contain brief OOC asides, archive the canon content
and ignore the aside.

entry. Preserve all proper nouns, character names, locations, factions, and specific
details. Do not use real-world timestamps. Do not editorialize or add information not in the message.
